## Notification APIs

- [Apprise](https://github.com/caronc/apprise) - Python library for sending notifications to a wide range of services including email, Slack, Discord, Telegram, and push services. Designed for scripts and self-hosted applications with a simple API. ([Read more](/details/apprise.md)) `Open Source` `API` `Python` `Multi-Channel`
- [Courier](https://courier.com/) - Multi-channel notification platform that helps product teams design, send, and track messages across email, SMS, push, and chat. - [Knock](https://knock.app/) - Developer-first notification API for building multi-channel product notifications with workflows, preferences, and batching. - [LogSnag](https://logsnag.com/) - Event tracking and notification platform for developers to send real-time alerts and logs to Slack, Discord, email, and mobile. - [Notify.Events](https://notify.events/) - Multi-channel notification aggregator that sends alerts from monitoring systems, servers, and apps to messengers and email. - [Shoutrrr](https://github.com/containrrr/shoutrrr) - Go notification library for sending messages to dozens of services from a single configuration. Intended for self-hosted tools and home automation with a simple API. ([Read more](/details/shoutrrr.md)) `Open Source` `API` `Go` `Self-Hosted`
- [SuprSend](https://suprsend.com/) - API-first notification infrastructure for product teams to manage transactional and marketing messages across channels. - [OneSignal](https://onesignal.com/) - Customer engagement platform specializing in mobile and web push notifications, in-app messages, and email. - [Pusher Beams](https://pusher.com/beams) - Push notification service for iOS and Android with device management, segmentation, and real-time delivery. - [Pushover](https://pushover.net/) - Simple push notification service for sending alerts to iOS, Android, and desktop devices via REST API. Requires a one-time license fee per platform with no monthly subscription. ([Read more](/details/pushover.md)) `API` `Paid` `Mobile Push` `Desktop`
- [Pushy](https://pushy.me/) - Reliable push notification service for iOS and Android with a REST API, delivery receipts, and scheduling. Offers a free developer plan and paid production plans based on device count. ([Read more](/details/pushy.md)) `API` `Freemium` `Free Plan` `Mobile Push`
